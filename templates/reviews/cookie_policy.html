Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2023

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember information about your visit, improving your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

FeedMe uses cookies to:

  • Authenticate users and maintain sessions
  • Remember preferences and settings
  • Analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • Deliver personalized content
  • Improve our services and marketing

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Type Purpose Duration
Essential Core functionality Session
Preferences Remember settings 1 year
Analytics Usage tracking 2 years
Marketing Ad personalization 6 months

4. Third-Party Cookies

We partner with third-party services that may set cookies, including:

  • Google Analytics for website analytics
  • Facebook Pixel for advertising
  • Hotjar for user behavior analysis
  • Stripe for payment processing

5. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through:

  • Browser settings (disable all or certain cookies)
  • Our cookie consent banner when first visiting
  • Third-party opt-out tools (e.g., Digital Advertising Alliance)

Note: Disabling essential cookies may impact website functionality.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our cookie practices change. Significant changes will be communicated through our website.

7. Contact Us

For questions about our cookie practices, contact us at:
privacy@feedme.com
