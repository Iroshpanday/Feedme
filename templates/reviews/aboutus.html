About feedMe

Turn Noise into Action.

At feedMe, we believe every piece of customer feedback holds the key to growth. Our AI-powered platform transforms raw opinions into structured, visual insights, helping businesses listen smarter, act faster, and improve continuously.

Our Mission

To redefine feedback intelligence by:

  • Guaranteeing authenticity with video reviews and verified responses.
  • Delivering dashboards that matter—no data headaches, just clear next steps.
  • Empowering teams to make customer-driven decisions with confidence.

Our Vision

To be the global leader in feedback-driven growth, where:

  • Every customer voice fuels innovation.
  • Trust is built through transparency.
